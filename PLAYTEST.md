# Icarus: what to try

**justkeepflying.com.** Each line says what to do, then what you should see. Tick it off as you go.

## Flying (the phoenix)
- [ ] **W / S** climb and dive, **A / D** steer, **Space** beats the wings. Climbing costs speed and diving gives it back.
- [ ] **Shift** goes faster. **Q / E** roll. Hold **W** and you go over the top in a loop.
- [ ] **New, hyper:** hold **Shift** flying straight until it charges. Once you're in hyper you can let go and steer, climb, loop and turn as you like. Press **Shift** again to drop out (on a phone or gamepad, the boost). Diving at the ground still throws you out.
- [ ] **/loop**, **/barrel**, **/immelmann** in the chat. Each trick plays by itself.
- [ ] Fly up to the sun and level off. Your wings burn. Climb steeply instead and you break through into space.
- [ ] **Space** low down lands. Tap **Space** by a lamp, a branch or a roof to perch there.

## On foot (the man)
- [ ] **/human** in the air. You fall like a man, belly-down at first, and faster head-down.
- [ ] Walk with **WASD** and jump with **Space**. In the sea you swim. Tap **Space** to become the bird again.
- [ ] Hold **Space** by a tree or a rock to chop it or mine it.
- [ ] **I** opens your bag, where you make things (a torch, tools).
- [ ] **/build**, then **1-8** to pick a piece, and place it.

## Getting around
- [ ] **M** opens the map. Click a place to be carried there, or double-click it to fly there yourself.
- [ ] **New:** a click on the map carries you anywhere on it, charted or not (after your first flight). Ground nobody has charted shows dim and grey, not black. In the 3D view (**R**) everyone flying is a little bird in their colour, flapping and leaving a fading trail, turned the way they're going; you're the orange one.
- [ ] **New, the Underworld map:** **M** down there opens on the Underworld alone. Click to be carried anywhere in it. Once you've been down once, switch to both worlds with **U** and click a place in the hills to be carried up (or the other way down).
- [ ] **U** on the map, or its pill, shows the Underworld as a 3D block under the ground.
- [ ] The small map sits in the corner. **Shift M** turns it, points it north, or hides it.
- [ ] **F1** opens the controls card. **?** asks Hermes how to do something.

## Places
- [ ] **New, the top of Olympus:** the summit is a broad high meadow of short grass that sways and parts as you walk, with pale rock and small white, blue and yellow flowers, about 300 m across. The front, from where you wake to the north lip, is left open so you look straight out at the drop. Walk off the lip and the north face falls sheer, about 160 m straight down before the mountain slopes away: a real leap. Where you wake there are no thrones or shrines now: six tall standards with pennants (one per game), cairns to the edge, and the Way running south either side of the house of Zeus.
- [ ] **New, the House of the Twelve:** behind the house of Zeus stands a great house, eight storeys on a podium, built round the needle on three sides so the needle is its tower. If you have no god yet, a marker ("THE HOUSE OF THE TWELVE · KNEEL AT A THRONE") leads you to its door. Go round Zeus's house, up the great stair, and in.
  - **Ground floor:** the Hall of the Twelve, two storeys high, with the twelve thrones in a row along the far wall (Space at one to pray, or to speak with your god). Hades sits apart by the door. West wing: the feast hall. East wing: the baths.
  - **2nd:** the library (west) and the Gate Room (east), where the wormhole to BRN now is.
  - **3rd and 4th (white walls, blue shutters):** a room for each god, in their colour, with their bed, their sign and the altar that takes their stars.
  - **5th and 6th (stone, timber balconies, tiled eaves):** the Great Dormitory, the cells, a common room with a fire, a refectory. Stand by any bed and press Space to sleep: the screen goes dark, the night passes, you get up with your fire full.
  - **7th:** observatory, treasury, aviary, map room. **8th:** the Aerie, an open loggia. **Roof:** blue domes, a bell tower, a windmill that turns, and the Leap, a board run out over the front of the house to fly from.
  - **Getting about:** a stair in each wing (doubling back all the way up), a gallery round the court on every floor, and terraces where each tier steps back. Doors show their names as you come near; a room says its name as you walk in. You can't climb its walls or walk through them.
  - **As a bird (bigger now: every storey is 12 m high):** fly in at the great door (24 m wide), down the hall and out into the court; across the hall through the gates in its east and west sides; down the Nave on the 5th (the great dormitory, two storeys high) from one end gate to the other; through the Eye, which crosses the Nave north to south; in at the south end of either wing and out through the court arches. Walls turn you, they don't kill you.
  - **Perching:** come down on any roof, terrace or balcony. The Great Balcony on the front of the 8th floor is 60 m wide for the biggest birds, and its parapet is broad enough to stand on.
  - **Wind stairs:** in each wing, by the stairs, every floor has a gold ring (up) and a blue ring (down). Stand on one and press Space to be carried a storey. Eight presses take you from the ground to the roof.
  - **Life:** windows light up at dusk (a few stay dark); twelve flags in the gods' colours fly from the roof; doves circle the domes by day; two fountains play before the great stair; the common room's chimney smokes; lanterns rise from the roof at night; the bell rings on the hour; an orrery turns in the observatory.
- [ ] **New, trinkets of the gods:** seventy things out of the old stories stand on marble stands all through the house and round it (a gold star and a name show over the ones near you). Three in each god's room are that god's: Zeus's thunderbolt, Athena's owl and aegis, Hermes's sandals. The rest are where they belong: Pandora's jar in the hall, the golden fleece and the Gorgon's head in the treasury, the wings of Daedalus in the Aerie, the masks on the amphitheatre's stage. Stand by one and press Space: it tells its story a line at a time and counts it ("12 OF 70 FOUND"). Ones you've found show a dim star. Find all seventy and everyone is told.
- [ ] **New, round the meadow:** five windmills with turning sails and a small chapel with a blue dome on the west side; the Amphitheatre on the east (eleven rows of seats in a half-round, a walk under a colonnade round the top, an altar burning in the middle, a stage with the two masks: walk up the rows, or come down on the rim as a bird); cypresses along the house; statues of the Twelve on the first terrace. Their names show as you come near.
- [ ] **New, games from where you wake:** stand by a standard and press Space to call that game for everyone flying: the ring race, king of the ring, the flame (tag), the fastest, the storm, the last flier. You have to have flown once first.
- [ ] **New, the Flight of the House:** a gold ring hangs off the end of the Leap. Fly through it and a clock starts: fifteen more rings, down the west side, round the back, up the court, through the hall and out of the great door, back through the Eye and up over the roof. Your best time is kept.
- [ ] **New, stars of the gods:** small lights in each god's colour go round over Olympus, high round the crown for the bird and low over the table where the man can walk into them. The low ones now drift over the House of the Twelve, along its roofs and terraces. Take one (you can carry three) and bring it to that god's pillar, or to the altar in their room in the house (3rd and 4th floors). The pillar fills with their light from foot to crown for everyone, and the shrine's bowl burns. Every third star to the same god sets the whole pillar on fire with thunder. Your own god's star refills your fire. The wrong god's pillar won't take it.
- [ ] **Crete:** the Labyrinth, Daedalus's tower, Minos's palace, the town and the harbour.
- [ ] **The seas:** reefs, sunken statues, and water that changes with depth and weather.
- [ ] **New, skimming:** over open sea you can come right down onto the water. Roll with **E** or **Q** and only that wingtip cuts the water: a blade of spray, a line in the water, steam, and the wing dragged up and back, turning you toward it. Belly down at speed (dive at it gently) and you skip like a stone. The tail leaves a thread, and the head dipped in hisses. Try it as the hawk (**/small**) and as a big bird too.
- [ ] **The Underworld:** the Styx and Charon's ferry, the dead (**Space** burns them back), blue lava and Tartarus.
- [ ] **Weather:** tornadoes, dust storms, snow squalls, valley mist. Fly through them.
- [ ] **New, live weather:** storms are places on a shared clock (everyone sees the same one), with tornadoes, anvil clouds, high cirrus and towering dust walls. From space, the home globe shows the same live clouds and storms.
- [ ] **Fire:** brush past trees as the phoenix and they catch. Your wings fan it.
- [ ] **Birds:** eleven kinds. Flocks land and take off. Fly close and they catch fire, fall, and you can eat the roast.

## Space and the planets
- [ ] Climb steeply out of the top of the sky, holding W. Near the top the sky holds you in the climb and you are in space in under 10 s. The world becomes a globe below you, with rings and moons.
- [ ] **New, first trip only:** your chosen god comes up beside you as a bird and leads you past a moon to a world you could dive into. It talks about what you did on Olympus. Type or speak to it. Steer away and it waits.
- [ ] **New:** press **C** in space for the film camera. Slow, it skims a world's surface; fast, it pulls out to long lenses against the stars. It cuts when a world comes up or goes by, when you cross the rings, and when you dive into a world.
- [ ] **New:** in space the bird wears a glass helmet. Its wings are held out like sails with a slow wave down them, and **Space** is one long rowing stroke that leaves a ring of sparks behind.
- [ ] **New:** press **Q** in space (on a phone, the **THE MAN** button) and the bird breaks into a swarm of blocks that settle into the man, in a white space suit with a glass helmet (your face inside) and his wings still on. Drifting, he floats with arms up and knees bent; going (**Shift**), he lies out flat and the jets on his pack glow blue. **Q** again turns him back into the bird. Diving home or into a world as the man turns you back into the bird.
- [ ] Hold **S** to dive back at the home globe. You fade back into the sky.
- [ ] **New, space has life:** drifts of rocks, from grit to moonlets, walled into mazes with doorways between home and its moons and round some worlds. Big rocks push you off; small ones get knocked aside or burn up. In hyper you smash straight through. Look for wrecks (columns, statue heads, burnt wings), and named wrecks where real players fell. Six lantern-lit lanes run out from home, phoenix flocks fly them, and gates in the drifts light up for everyone when anyone flies through.
- [ ] **New, friends in space:** other players in space show up as birds in their own colour with their names.
- [ ] In space, **M** opens the map of the heavens: drag it, pinch it, pick a world.
- [ ] **New:** on the map of the heavens, click a world to go round it, then click it again to be carried there, set down just outside it, facing it.
- [ ] **New, slowing in space:** hold **X** (on a phone, hold **SLOW**) and you slow to a drift; let go to fly on. Saying "slow down" or "stop" does it too; a wingbeat (**Space**) or "faster" goes again.
- [ ] Dive at any world. **New:** the screen fills with the colour of its sky, and the world comes up out of the mist.
- [ ] Try all nine:
  - rock
  - ice
  - fire (lava feeds the bird but burns the man)
  - NEPHELE, the gas giant, with its cloud islands
  - ocean
  - desert
  - forest
  - crystal
  - poison
- [ ] On each world, follow the thread of light to the thing to find there.
- [ ] Climb straight up to leave. **New:** the sky goes dark and space fades in around you, with no flash.

## Meteora (your agents)
- [ ] Tiny monks on the sandstone rocks build monasteries with winches, cranes and levers.
- [ ] Walk the rope bridges and perch on them. Burn one as the phoenix and watch the monks weave it back.
- [ ] **/hamlet** lists your agents and marks the way to them.

## Gods and story
- [ ] Walk up to a god and speak or type. Be rude and Zeus smites you.
- [ ] **J** shows quests and **Z** your stats.
- [ ] **New, voice:** say "quests", "stats", "controls", "map", "close", "slow down", and in space "go to" and a world's name.
- [ ] **New, god mode:** after it ends you can call it again once you land and rest, or after 15 s anywhere (in the air, in space, in the Underworld). The man in space becomes the bird when it starts.
- [ ] **B**, or say "become a whale", takes any shape.

## Playing with others
- [ ] **T** chat, **Tab** who's on, **V** mic.
- [ ] **R**, or blow into the mic, leaves a wind anyone can ride.
- [ ] **/fly** starts a fight. **F** fire, **R** wind, **C / 1-8** shapes. The **/fly laser** cuts people in half.
- [ ] Deaths show in the chat ("X flew too close to the sun").
- [ ] Two people on the same planet see each other, and are hidden from everyone at home.

## Cameras
- [ ] **C** cinematic camera (chase, wing mount, IMAX wide). Director mode: describe a shot by voice or text.
- [ ] **New:** in the cinematic camera, the bird's wings and body break out over the black bars, as if coming out of the screen.

## The map
- [ ] **New:** on the flat map the **3D** button (top right), the title and the place names are clearly visible. **R** or the button switches between flat and 3D, both ways, on desktop and phone.
- [ ] **F**, outside a fight, opens the photo camera. Photos go to the album.

## Devices (never tested on real hardware, so worth a look)
- [ ] **Phone:** touch and tilt, the one ICARUS menu button, two fingers to blow wind.
- [ ] **New, phone sharpness:** letters are drawn on the phone's own pixels (3x on most phones), so everything should look crisper. If it gets choppy, it drops back to 2x by itself.
- [ ] **Gamepad.**
- [ ] **Vision Pro:** hand gestures and voice.
- [ ] **With friends:** real multiplayer, fights and planets together.
